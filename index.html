The problem

Your hospital is already paying
for readmissions it could prevent.

Under the Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program, CMS penalizes hospitals with excess 30-day readmission rates—up to 3% of total Medicare payments. At Baptist Health South Florida, readmissions climbed from a 19.7% target to 28% in Q2 2025—42% above goal. The most affected: lower-income households, Medicaid enrollees, and communities of color.

Our solution

One platform. Three critical capabilities.

We don’t just flag risk—we close the loop from identification through intervention and monitoring.

01

Predictive risk intelligence

Proprietary algorithms analyze clinical data, utilization patterns, and social determinants to stratify patients at admission. Integrates with Epic, Cerner, and MEDITECH.

02

Multilingual patient assessments

Patient-facing tools in 12+ languages capture what EHRs miss: transportation barriers, food insecurity, medication comprehension, and caregiver availability.

03

Automated care coordination

Auto-generates discharge plans, connects patients to community resources, and triggers post-discharge follow-ups. CMS audit-ready reporting built in.
