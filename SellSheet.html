The Problem

Hospitals face CMS penalties up to 3% of Medicare reimbursements when patients return within 30 days. At Baptist Health South Florida, readmissions climbed from a 19.7% target to 28% in Q2 2025 — 42% above goal. Most affected: lower-income households, Medicaid enrollees, and communities of color. Current tools miss these patients because they ignore Social Determinants of Health and fail non-English speakers.

The Solution

VulnerablePatients screens for the social and behavioral barriers that clinical data alone misses — housing stability, food access, medication understanding, and caregiver support — in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole, before discharge. Results route patients to insurance-specific resources and actionable next steps.
